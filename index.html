• Azra Begzadić
I am a Ph.D. student in Mechanical and Aerospace Engineering at UC San Diego, where I am advised by Prof. Jorge Cortés and Prof. Sylvia Herbert. My research interests lie in safety-critical learning-based control. During my Ph.D., I interned at Toyota Research Institute in the Human Interactive Driving group, where I worked on learning-based control for safe autonomous driving. Before joining UC San Diego, I received my M.Sc. from the Technical University of Munich, Germany, in 2023, and my B.Sc. from the Vienna University of Technology, Austria, in 2020, both in electrical engineering and information technology.
